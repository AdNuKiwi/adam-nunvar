The two projects completed for my field placements, KIN*3090 and KIN*3190.

Winter 2026KIN*3090, Field Placement I

Chronic Shoulder Tendinopathy Home Exercise Program

The Spot Physiotherapy Rehab & Performance

I created a branded, 12-slide guide on chronic rotator cuff tendinopathy, a very common condition at the clinic. It was structured as a four-phase home exercise program, starting with pain management and restoring range of motion before progressing to strengthening. I learned how to translate physiology into a version a client would understand, a skill I have kept developing ever since.

The project’s impact was limited: it was designed mainly to show my supervisor my understanding of shoulder conditions, and it likely never reached any clients. That taught me a valuable lesson, and I made sure my next placement project would create real, lasting impact.

Skills built: client education, exercise prescription, visual communication

Late November 2026KIN*3190, Field Placement II

Active Aging Workshop Series In progress

Town of East Gwillimbury

For my current placement, I am developing my own registered program: a three-part workshop series on active aging for older adults in the area, planned for three Tuesday mornings in late November. Each session will open with a 15-minute presentation on a practical topic, such as strength and bone health, balance, or mobility, all tying into long-term functional independence, followed by a mini workout that applies what was discussed. The Town will promote the series as one of its own.

With this project, I am combining what I have learned in lecture with what I have learned running my business, and presenting it myself to a real audience. Beyond the impact on the people attending, the Town may see more sign-ups for its fitness programs and may run something similar in the future.

Skills built: program design, public speaking, knowledge translation
