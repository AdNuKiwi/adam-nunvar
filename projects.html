Chronic Shoulder Tendinopathy Home Exercise Program

The Spot Physiotherapy Rehab & Performance

Winter 2026KIN*3090, Field Placement I

Three of the 12 slides

What I made

A branded, 12-slide home exercise program for chronic rotator cuff tendinopathy, a very common condition at the clinic, organized into four phases from pain management and range of motion through to strengthening.

Impact

The program received positive feedback from my supervisor and demonstrated a strong understanding of chronic shoulder tendinopathy. It was designed to showcase my knowledge rather than as a client handout, so it was not distributed to clients.

What it taught me

How to translate physiology into language a client can understand, a skill I have kept developing ever since. It also taught me to make sure my next placement project would reach real people.

Skills built: client education, exercise prescription, phased program design, visual communication

Active Aging Workshop Series In progress

Town of East Gwillimbury

Late November 2026KIN*3190, Field Placement II

Opening slide from each workshop

What I am building

My own registered program: a three-part workshop series on active aging for older adults, running on Tuesday mornings in late November. Each session pairs a 15-minute presentation on strength and bone health, balance, or mobility with a short workout that puts it into practice. The series is on the Town’s official program calendar with 15 spots to fill, and I am promoting it myself by pitching it at the start of the Town’s current fitness classes.

Expected impact

Practical tools that help participants stay independent for longer, more sign-ups for the Town’s fitness programs, and a format the Town could run again in the future.

What it is teaching me

How to combine what I have learned in lectures with my experience running a business, and how to present that to a real audience.

Skills built: program design, public speaking, knowledge translation, group exercise instruction, program promotion
