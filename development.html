Summer Company Program

York Small Business Enterprise Centre

Summer 2026York Region

Received a $3,000 startup grant to launch Mount Wellness and completed workshops in marketing, bookkeeping, AI, leadership, and branding.

The biggest lesson I took from the program is that business is all about people. People have to like and trust you, and that shapes everything from branding to how you deliver your service. Most of my clients have come through referrals or heard my name from someone who already liked working with me. Being honest, genuine, present, and polite turned out to be far more valuable than I expected, and I now put that first in every part of my business.
