May 2026 – presentMount Albert, ON

Founder

Mount Wellness, small-group fitness for adults 60+

Building this business has helped me grow tremendously, both as a person and a professional. Since launching in May 2026, 22 people have registered, and I now run two classes a week with 10 to 12 regular clients. The business is profitable on its own, even without the $3,000 startup grant. Along with the usual startup skills, I also became a more compassionate and caring person through my clients. One client told me that, for the first time, she walked up a certain hill without taking a break, and I could feel how much that meant to her. Moments like that have shown me how much a small improvement can change someone’s life.

Sept 2026 – presentEast Gwillimbury, ON

Recreation Placement Student (KIN*3190)

Town of East Gwillimbury, Recreation Department

My placement in the Town’s recreation department has let me look at fitness from an entirely different perspective. Before this, I had only worked with people one-on-one or in small groups. Now I get to see behind the scenes of a municipality that delivers fitness and health programs to thousands of people across multiple facilities. I have gotten significantly better at two things. First, I have a much better grasp of how a large organization plans and runs its programs, which I put to use in a gap analysis comparing East Gwillimbury’s drop-in programs to those of other towns. Second, I have become a much better team player, working closely with the recreation team on everything from inventory checks to preparing materials for the Senior Health and Wellness Fair. I have also substituted some of their classes and am building a registered program of my own.

Skills built: program planning, gap analysis, teamwork, class instruction

Jan – Mar 2026Markham, ON

Rehabilitation Practitioner Assistant (KIN*3090)

The Spot Physiotherapy Rehab & Performance

This placement gave me critical insight into what rehabilitative kinesiology looks like in practice. I interacted with patients, shadowed treatments, demonstrated prescribed exercises (most often hip, lower back, and shoulder strengthening, plus mobility work), and applied modalities including heat, pneumatic compression boots, heat-and-vibration devices, and hot/cold compression therapy. The real value of this experience was discovering that a clinical rehab setting was not the right fit for me. That realization is what led me to start my own business, where I still get to connect with people, but with much more interesting problems to solve.

Skills built: exercise demonstration, therapeutic modalities, patient interaction
