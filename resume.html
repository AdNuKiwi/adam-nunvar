May 2026 – presentMount Albert, ON

Founder

Mount Wellness, small-group fitness for adults 60+

Building this business has helped me grow, both as a person and as a professional. Sept 2026 – presentEast Gwillimbury, ON

Recreation Placement Student (KIN*3190)

Town of East Gwillimbury, Recreation Department

This placement has shown me fitness from an entirely different perspective. Before it, I had only worked with people one-on-one or in small groups. Now I see behind the scenes of a municipality that runs fitness and health programs for thousands of people across multiple facilities. I have gotten noticeably better at two things. First, I understand how a large organization plans and runs its programs, which I put to use in a gap analysis comparing East Gwillimbury’s drop-in programs to those of other towns. Second, I have become a much better team player, working closely with the recreation staff on everything from inventory checks to preparing materials for the Senior Health and Wellness Fair. I have also covered some of their classes and am building a registered program of my own.

Skills built: program planning, gap analysis, teamwork, class instruction

Jan – Mar 2026Markham, ON

Rehabilitation Practitioner Assistant (KIN*3090)

The Spot Physiotherapy Rehab & Performance

This placement showed me what rehabilitative kinesiology looks like in practice. I worked with patients, shadowed treatments, demonstrated prescribed exercises (most often hip, lower back, and shoulder strengthening, plus mobility work), and applied modalities including heat, compression boots, heat-and-vibration devices, and hot and cold compression therapy. Its biggest value was showing me that a clinical rehab setting is not the right fit for me. Most patients come in with similar issues, so the day-to-day work can become quite repetitive, and I wanted more variety in the problems I solve. That realization is what pushed me to start my own business, where I still connect with people but face new challenges every day.

Skills built: exercise demonstration, therapeutic modalities, patient interaction
