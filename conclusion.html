Conclusion

Through hard work and a hunger for new challenges, I have built experience that is rare for an undergraduate student: a profitable business started from scratch and over two years of training clients, all while maintaining a 93.71% average.

The biggest thing I have learned is that there is a reciprocal relationship between my personal and professional development. Working with older adults has made me more patient and compassionate, and those qualities have made me a better coach. In turn, presenting at events, pitching my services, and leading my own classes have made me a more confident person outside of work, too.

My next step is becoming a Registered Kinesiologist, then continuing to grow Mount Wellness through partnerships with municipalities and retirement residences and offering one-on-one training in my community.
